# Project Charter

## Product

Youth Ministry Website V1

## Vision

Create a trustworthy digital home for the Youth Ministry: a place where young people can discover the community, encounter useful spiritual content, learn about events, watch sermons, hear stories, and take a clear next step to connect.

## Product outcomes

1. A new visitor understands the ministry and can find a next step within seconds.
2. Returning visitors can find fresh articles, sermons, events, and resources.
3. Ministry staff can publish and update content through a CMS without code changes.
4. Social channels can link to stable, shareable, SEO-friendly website pages.
5. The technical foundation can scale in content volume, traffic, contributors, and future features.

## Primary audiences

- Young people already connected to the ministry
- Young people discovering the ministry for the first time
- Parents, leaders, churches, and partners who need trustworthy information
- Content/media/ministry staff operating the website

## Product principles

- Gospel/ministry purpose before novelty
- Authentic people and stories before stock-like visuals
- Mobile first
- Content first
- Clear next actions
- Fast by default
- Accessible by default
- Privacy by default
- Simple operations over clever engineering

## V1 success indicators

Measure after launch:
- content can be published without developer help;
- key pages are indexed and share correctly;
- visitors can find events, sermons, articles, and contact/join information;
- no critical accessibility/security defects;
- performance budgets are met for representative mobile conditions;
- content team is actively operating the publishing workflow.

Do not invent numeric growth targets until the ministry has baseline analytics.
